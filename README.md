
# 🌙 NightCare – Smart Hospital Night-Shift Assistance System

NightCare is a smart hospital assistance platform designed to improve communication between patients, attendants, nurses, and hospital administrators, particularly during night shifts.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Patients and their attendants often experience difficulties contacting nursing staff during nighttime hospital stays. Delayed communication, staff availability issues, and difficulty locating responsible staff can affect the patient experience.

NightCare aims to address these challenges through a centralized digital assistance platform.

## 💡 Key Features

- Patient and attendant assistance requests
- Real-time nurse availability tracking
- Smart nurse assignment
- Instant notifications
- Automatic request escalation
- Hospital ward and room management
- Nurse shift management
- Hospital administration dashboard
- Request acknowledgement and completion tracking

## 🏗️ Project Status

🚧 Currently under development.

Initial development focuses on the frontend interface, patient experience, nurse dashboard, and administrator dashboard.

## 🔐 Safety & Privacy

NightCare is intended to complement existing hospital communication systems, not replace emergency call systems or clinical decision-making.

Patient information and staff data must be protected through appropriate authentication, authorization, and privacy controls.
